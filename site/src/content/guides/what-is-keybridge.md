KeyBridge lets you use your **phone as a keyboard for your computer**. You type on your phone, and the words appear on your PC — as if you were typing on a keyboard plugged into it.

It works over your home Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the internet, and you don't need an account.

## What can I do with it?

- **Type and paste text** onto your PC from your phone — handy when the PC is across the room, mounted on a wall, or plugged into a TV.
- **Send keyboard shortcuts** like copy, paste, alt-tab, or volume up.
- **Control media** — play, pause, skip, and change volume on the PC.
- **Paste long things** — a wall of text or a web address sends in one go, with a progress bar so you can see it arrive.

## What do I need?

Two free pieces:

1. **The KeyBridge app** on your Android phone.
2. **The KeyBridge Server** on your Windows PC — a small program that receives what you type and types it for you.

Both need to be on the **same Wi-Fi network**. That's the only requirement.

## How does it connect?

When you run the server on your PC, it shows a **QR code**. You scan that code with the app, and the two are paired. After that, whatever you type on the phone shows up on the PC.

> You only scan the QR code once per session. Pairing lasts until you close the server.

## Is it complicated?

No. The PC program is a double-click install — no command line, no setup files to edit. The app is a normal install from a signed APK. If you can scan a QR code, you can use KeyBridge.
