Once your phone is paired (see [getting started](/KeyBridge/guides/getting-started)), the app is more than a text box. It has a **control grid** of real keyboard keys you can tap.

## Media controls

Tap to control whatever is playing on your PC — a video, music, a browser tab:

- **Play / Pause**
- **Next** and **Previous** track
- **Volume up / down** and **Mute**

These are the same media keys found on a full-size keyboard, so they work with most players and streaming sites.

## Keyboard shortcuts and combos

The grid also has the keys you reach for most:

- **Modifiers** — Ctrl, Alt, Shift, and the Windows key.
- **Navigation** — arrows, Home, End, Page Up/Down, Tab, Esc.
- **Function keys** — F1 through F12.

You can build **combos** by holding a modifier and tapping another key — for example Alt+Tab to switch windows, or Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V to copy and paste. They're sent as genuine keystrokes, so the PC reacts exactly as if you pressed them yourself.

## Handy setups

- **Living-room PC:** prop the phone next to you and use it as the remote for a media-center PC plugged into the TV.
- **Presentations:** use the arrow keys to advance slides from across the room.
- **Long text:** paste a URL or a block of text into a remote machine without hunting for its keyboard.

## A note on Mac

If your computer is a Mac, turn on **Mac mode** in the app's settings. The key labels switch to match macOS (Command instead of the Windows key, and so on) so the combos do what you expect.
